# Business Profile — Apex Decking Co (EXAMPLE — fictional business)

> Example of a completed profile, for testing agents and showing buyers what good looks
> like. Every figure below is invented.

*Last updated: 2026-07-31 · Updated by: Advisor first-run intake*

## 1. What the business is

- **Business name:** Apex Decking Co
- **Line of work (plain words):** residential decking + outdoor living builder
- **Who buys:** homeowners 35–65, renovating, house value $900K+, northern Brisbane suburbs
- **Service area / market:** Brisbane north, ~40 min radius
- **What an average customer is worth:** first job ~$18,000 · lifetime ~$24,000 (est — repeat/extension work, unmeasured)
- **The offer in one sentence:** custom hardwood decks from $12K, fixed-price quote in 48
  hours, 10-year workmanship warranty

## 2. The numbers

- **Revenue band (monthly):** ~$75K
- **Rough gross margin:** ~35%
- **Sales model:** quoted jobs (site visit → quote → follow-up)
- **Close rate (lead → customer):** owner says "maybe 1 in 4 that we quote" — `unknown` measured
- **Current cost per lead:** `unknown` — "the Facebook guy sends a report but I don't read it"

## 3. Where customers come from today

| Channel | Active? | Monthly spend | Working? (owner's honest read) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referrals / word of mouth | Yes | $0 | "Our best jobs, but unpredictable" |
| Paid social (Meta) | Yes — via freelancer | $2,500 | "Leads come in but heaps are tyre-kickers" |
| Google (ads / maps / SEO) | Maps listing only, unclaimed reviews | $0 | "Never looked at it" |
| Outbound | No | — | — |
| Content / socials | Sporadic IG posts | $0 | "When we remember" |

## 4. What happens to a lead

- **First hour:** lead form email lands in the owner's inbox; he calls back "usually same
  day, sometimes a couple of days if we're on the tools"
- **Who responds:** owner, from his mobile
- **CRM in use:** none — inbox + a notebook in the ute
- **Automated follow-up:** none
- **Quiet leads:** "if they don't answer twice I move on"

## 5. Team & time

- **Marketing/sales hours:** owner only, ~3 hrs/week, evenings
- **Owner's hours in the business:** ~60/week (on the tools 4 days)
- **Appetite:** wants it handled — will follow a system but won't build one

## 6. Constraints & compliance

- **Regulated claims:** none (QBCC licence number must appear on ads — standard)
- **Hard constraints:** can take max 4 new jobs/month at current crew size; won't do
  jobs under $8K

## 7. Confirmed learnings

- 2026-07-31 — Advisor — close rate and CPL both unmeasured; tracking is a Level-2 finding

## 8. Owner's own read

- **What the owner thinks the bottleneck is:** "I just need better quality leads —
  Facebook sends me dreamers"
- **Stage read (Advisor's note):** *feels it but misdiagnosed* — blames lead quality
  (Level 4) while Level 2 (no numbers) and Level 3 (same-day-at-best callback, no chase,
  no CRM) are failing above it. Classic "more leads" ask with a leaking bucket.
